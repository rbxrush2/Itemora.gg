Frequently Asked Questions

Is Itemora a trusted platform?

Yes — Itemora is designed to offer a safe and smooth digital shopping experience. All payments go through secure third-party processors, and no sensitive data is stored on our servers.

How fast is delivery?

Most orders are completed within minutes depending on the game and item. During peak hours or server delays, delivery may take slightly longer.

What is your refund policy?

Due to the nature of digital goods, refunds are not possible once an item has been delivered. Crypto payments are strictly non-refundable. However, if you face a genuine issue, contact us and we’ll help resolve it.

Are you affiliated with Roblox or any game developers?

No — Itemora is an independent platform. We are not endorsed by Roblox, Big Games, Uplift Games, or any other developers. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

What if I don’t receive my items?

In rare situations, if delivery is delayed, simply open a ticket on our Discord server. Our support team usually responds quickly and ensures you get your purchase.
